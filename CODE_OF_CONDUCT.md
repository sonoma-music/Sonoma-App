# Code of Conduct

Sonoma is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or chat — is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with newcomers;
- disagree about ideas, never attack people;
- assume good intent and ask before assuming the worst;
- keep feedback specific and constructive;
- respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual content, doxxing, or encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
