# Library & playback

## How the library is built

When you add a folder, Sonoma scans it and reads each file's tags: title, artist, album, album artist, year, track number, duration and embedded cover art. Files without tags use their file name, so `Artist - Title.mp3` becomes artist "Artist" and title "Title".

Songs are grouped into albums by album artist and album name. Your library, playlists and settings are saved in one file:

- Windows: `%APPDATA%\sonoma\sonoma.json`
- Linux: `~/.config/sonoma/sonoma.json`

Sonoma never moves, renames or deletes your music files. **Remove from library** and **Clear library** only make Sonoma forget them.

## Albums and playlists

- Open an album from Home or Search. **Play all** plays it in order; the shuffle button next to it plays it shuffled.
- **Search in album**, **Add to queue**, sort (track number, title, artist, duration) and the **⋯** menu sit on the right.
- Create a playlist from **Library → New playlist**, or from any song's **⋮** menu → **New playlist…**.
- Heart a song in the player bar to add it to **Favorites**.

## Song menu

Right-click a song, or use its **⋮** button, to:

- play it, play it next or add it to the queue;
- add it to favorites or a playlist;
- go to its album;
- copy a smart link;
- **Fix metadata…** (see [Get music](get-music.md));
- remove it from a playlist or from the library.

## Playback

- **Shuffle** randomises the queue; **repeat** cycles through off, all and one.
- Click or drag the thin progress line at the top of the player bar to seek.
- The queue panel (rightmost button) shows what's next. Click a song to jump to it.
- The background colour follows the cover art of what you're looking at.
