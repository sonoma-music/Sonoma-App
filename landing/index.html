Paste any link

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Deezer, Tidal or SoundCloud. Sonoma identifies the song and checks your library.

Buy the lossless file

One click to Qobuz, Bandcamp, 7digital or HDtracks. You own the file and the artist gets paid.

Free & legal sources

Internet Archive and Jamendo: artist-approved live sets, public domain and Creative Commons, often in FLAC.

Batch downloads

Queue whole albums with progress, retry and cancel. Files land in Artist / Album folders.

Metadata done for you

Tags from MusicBrainz, art from Cover Art Archive and lyrics from LRCLIB, written into every file. Fix old files too.

Rip CDs & watch folders

Rip discs to FLAC on Linux, and auto-import anything you buy elsewhere by watching your downloads folder.

Sonoma never downloads from streaming services. It only uses sources that allow downloading, and links you to stores for everything else.

No telemetry by default

Crash reports are opt-in. Your library, file paths and listening history never leave your computer.

No ads, ever

No pre-rolls, banners or sponsored playlists. Nothing between you and your music.

Minimal data, by design

No account needed. Your profile and settings are stored in a single local file.

Fast on old machines

Tags are read once and cached, covers are kept small, and switching views is instant.

/faq

Common questions

Is Sonoma completely free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads, no paid plans and no account. Sonoma is open source under the MIT licence.

Can I import my playlists from Spotify?

Yes. Paste a playlist link (you add your own free Spotify developer keys in Settings) or open an M3U file. Songs are matched to the files in your library.

Can Sonoma download from Spotify or Tidal?

No. Sonoma identifies the song from your link, then shows stores where you can buy the lossless file and free sources that allow downloading, such as Internet Archive and Jamendo.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10 and 11, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux (AppImage), Android 7+ and iPhone/iPad with iOS 14+. The phone apps play the music you add to them; CD ripping and watch folders are desktop-only.

Does Sonoma collect my listening data?

No. Nothing is sent unless you opt in to crash reports. Lyrics are looked up on LRCLIB by song title and artist only.

How do listening rooms work?

Start a room server with one command (or use a friend's), create a room and share the code. The host picks the music and everyone stays in sync.

Is Sonoma open source?

Yes, under the MIT licence. You can read the code, build it yourself and run your own room server.
